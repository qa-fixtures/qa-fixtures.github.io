Tenebris Refining Assurance Programme — conformant facilities

The Tenebris Refining Assurance Programme is a fictional register published as QA test data. It is not a real programme. It lists smelters and refiners that took part in an independent third-party assessment, with the facility's country and its assessment status. Only current participants are listed; a facility that left the programme is removed. The list names facilities only: it does not state which company operates or owns a facility.

Statuses: Conformant — the facility passed its most recent assessment; Active — the facility committed to an assessment that is not yet complete.
