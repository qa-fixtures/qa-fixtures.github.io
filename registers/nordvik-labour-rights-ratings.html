Nordvik Labour Rights Monitor — country ratings

The Nordvik Labour Rights Monitor is a fictional register published as QA test data. It is not a real index. It rates countries on the same 1 to 5+ scale as the ITUC Global Rights Index, where 1 means sporadic violations of rights and 5+ means no guarantee of rights due to the breakdown of the rule of law. This page does not state a year or an edition.
