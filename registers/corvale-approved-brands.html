Corvale Metals Exchange — approved cobalt brands

The Corvale Metals Exchange is a fictional exchange published as QA test data. It is not a real exchange. This page lists the cobalt brands approved for delivery against its cobalt contract. Each row names the brand, the company that produces it, the producer's country and the brand's listing status. Only brands currently on the list are shown; a delisted brand is removed.

Statuses: Approved — the brand is good for delivery; Suspended — the brand is temporarily not good for delivery.
