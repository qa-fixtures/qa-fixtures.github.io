Halvorsen Silica Works AS
Responsible Sourcing Policy 2026 (plain-text edition)
Internal reference: HSW-RSP-4417

1. Purpose
Halvorsen Silica Works AS publishes this Responsible Sourcing Policy to set out how the company selects, audits and exits suppliers of quartz, woodchips and electrodes for its Brennvik plant.

2. Commitments
The company commits to source 100 percent of its quartz from quarries that have completed an OECD Annex II risk assessment. In the 2025 reporting year, 91 percent of quartz tonnage came from quarries holding a current third-party audit. Every electrode supplier signs the Halvorsen Supplier Code before its first delivery.

3. Grievance mechanism
Any worker, community member or supplier may raise a concern through the Halvorsen Ethics Line, which is operated by an independent provider and accepts reports in Norwegian, English, French and Portuguese. In 2025 the Ethics Line received 9 reports; 8 were closed within 60 days.

4. Déclaration de conformité (résumé en français)
Halvorsen Silica Works AS déclare que la présente politique s'applique à l'ensemble de ses fournisseurs de quartz. Référence interne : HSW-RSP-4417. Les écarts constatés lors des audits sont traités dans un délai de quatre-vingt-dix jours, et le comité d'éthique en rend compte chaque trimestre.

5. Review
This policy is reviewed every two years by the Board Sustainability Committee. The next review is scheduled for April 2028.
