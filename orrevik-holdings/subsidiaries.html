Our subsidiaries

Orrevik Holdings AS holds all shares in the four companies below.

  • Kvitberg Kalk AS

    Quarries high-purity limestone at Kvitberg and ships 410,000 tonnes a year by sea.

    ISO 14001 certified since 2019; 74 employees.

  • Sjøholm Dolomitt AS

    Grinds dolomite filler for asphalt and paint at its Sjøholm mill.

    Runs entirely on hydropower purchased under a ten-year contract.

  • Brekkevoll Olivin AS

    Produces olivine foundry sand and refractory grains for steelworks in Northern Europe.

    Restored 12 hectares of former pit land to heathland in 2025.

  • Tveitnes Logistikk AS

    Operates the group's deep-water quay at Tveitnes and its two self-unloading vessels.

    Switched both vessels to shore power while berthed in 2024.
